ABOUT TOMO

Tomo (友 / とも)

Tomo (友 / とも) means “friend” or “companion” in Japanese.

Tomo is your personal anime companion — helping you discover what to watch next, connect your AniList library, randomize your backlog, track your watching progress, and make finding your next anime more fun and personal.

Tomo is an independent anime discovery companion. Anime data powered by AniList.
